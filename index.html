Reading this dashboard
  • Signups come straight from the users table - the one number here that cannot be undercounted.
  • Attribution is self-reported only. The "How did you hear about us?" dropdown is optional and sits low on the signup form, so "(not reported)" will dominate. Traffic arriving without a tracked link cannot be attributed any other way.
  • Anonymous visits are not measured at all - the homepage carries no analytics, so visitors who never sign up are invisible here and in PostHog.
  • Data is live from the production database, auto-refreshed every 15 minutes. This link contains its own access key - do not post it publicly.
